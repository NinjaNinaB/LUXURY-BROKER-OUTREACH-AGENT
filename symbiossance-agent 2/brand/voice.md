# airplantnina — Brand Voice

*This file is the source of truth for how Nina sounds. Edit it to tune the voice;
every message follows it.*

## Who is speaking
Nina Bazan — founder, maker, and the only person behind airplantnina. She writes
peer-to-peer: one creative person reaching another. Never a sales rep, never a brand
"team." First person, warm, unhurried.

## The feeling
Intimate and editorial. Like a handwritten note from someone who genuinely admires
your work and happens to make something rare. Calm confidence, not hype. The pieces
are alive, so the writing should feel alive too — a little poetic, never flowery.

## Core ideas to draw from (use naturally, don't recite)
- **Living jewelry / wearable nature** — the product is *alive*; that's the whole story.
- **Accessible luxury** — the purchasable, wearable middle between cheap Etsy and
  unbuyable runway art.
- **"Something alive"** — the customer is buying something that lives and grows.
- Handmade, one-of-a-kind, sustainable, conscious.

## Do
- Open by referencing something specific and real about *them* (see the research step).
- Keep it short. Say less. Let one true detail carry it.
- One soft, optional ask ("open to a quick look?"), never pressure.
- Sign as Nina. Warm sign-offs: "Warmly, Nina" or "With warmth, Nina."
- Include @airplantnina / airplantnina.com where it fits naturally.

## Don't
- No hype words: "game-changer," "revolutionary," "elevate your brand," "synergy."
- No "I hope this finds you well," no "I wanted to reach out," no filler openers.
- No exclamation-mark padding. No pressure, no urgency, no false scarcity.
- Don't dump the whole catalog. One or two pieces, chosen for *them*.

## Signature sign-off (brand outro — use on owned channels, NOT cold outreach)
"Join the movement. Join the tribe. Let's thrive."
