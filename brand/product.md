# airplantnina — Product Facts

## What it is
Luxury wearable pieces and installations made from **real, living air plants
(Tillandsia)** — "living jewelry." Collars, headpieces, sculptural earrings, and
larger installation/runway pieces. Each piece is **handmade and one-of-a-kind**.

## Price
Retail $180–$480. Wholesale ~50% of SRP (see industry_agreements_research.md).

## Why it's different
It's *alive.* Not a print, not a charm shaped like a plant — a living organism the
wearer keeps and tends. The conversation piece is the life in it.

## Care (so claims are accurate)
- **No soil, no water tray.** Tillandsia are epiphytes — they live on air.
- Mist 1–2x per week; occasional soak; bright, indirect light.
- Lasts years with minimal care.
- Every piece ships with a care card and airplantnina-branded packaging.

## Sustainability / values
Living, low-resource, handmade, slow-made. Conscious luxury — for buyers and
wearers who care about where things come from and that they last.

## What it is NOT
Not mass-market, not dropship, not preserved/fake plants, not a quick Etsy charm.
