# airplantnina — Positioning

## The one-line
The purchasable, wearable answer to living-plant fashion — **accessible luxury**
between cheap Etsy plant jewelry and unbuyable runway plant art.

## The gap we own
- Below us: Etsy sellers (Lady of the Leaf, Easygrowindesigns, Lush Lobes) — cheap,
  generic, no brand story or editorial presence.
- Above us: Paula Ulargui Escalona — living-plant fashion as art, not for sale, not
  wearable daily.
- **The middle is empty. airplantnina fills it.** Premium, editorial, and actually
  buyable + wearable.

## First wedge customer (wholesale)
Buyer/owner of a high-end botanical, concept, or sustainable-luxury boutique who
curates distinctive, living, story-worthy pieces. First verifiable yes = replies and
asks for a line sheet.

## Two outreach modes (same engine)
- **Wholesale:** boutique buyers + gift brokers → a wholesale partnership.
- **Gifting:** celebrity stylists (reached, not the celebrity) → a no-obligation
  gift + a press/placement moment. Dream-client archetype: conscious + elevated +
  current (e.g. SZA, Billie Eilish).

## Collaboration channels (not competitors)
Sustainability creators like Climatediva are seeding/gifting partners, not rivals.
